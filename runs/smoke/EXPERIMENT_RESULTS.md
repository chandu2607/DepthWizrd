## What this run does and does NOT prove

- It measures whether a **small learned RGB+depth fusion head beats trivial affine calibration on a fully held-out city**, using DFC2019 (US WorldView-3) imagery.
- It does **not** establish accuracy on **Indian ISRO optical imagery** (the final evaluation domain). Cross-*city* generalization within DFC2019 is a necessary-but-insufficient proxy; cross-*sensor/country* shift is the top open risk and is out of scope for Phase 1.
- Absolute nDSM error does **not** include DTM error; a full DSM adds terrain from a DEM, whose own error compounds. Not evaluated here.
