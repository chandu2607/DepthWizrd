### §31 Connection to prior model failures

- **~14 m ceiling** — *consistent with* a metric depth→height scale shift: a single global slope fit on JAX cannot reach OMA's tall heights when OMA's oracle slope differs systematically. The shift *supports* the ceiling being a scale-reach limit, not a capacity limit (Phase-5 already *refuted* receptive field).
- **log1p (Phase-2)** — *does not explain* the cross-city shift; it reshaped the target tail but left the per-scene slope mismatch untouched — *consistent with* its WASH headline.
- **Aggressive / gentle loss weighting (Phase-3/4)** — *consistent with* over-correction/averaging artifacts: re-weighting a globally-miscalibrated slope trades error between bins without fixing the scene-scale mismatch.
- **Larger receptive field (Phase-5)** — *does not explain* the tall tail; forensics *support* the bottleneck being scene-scale, not spatial context.
- **Adaptive-scale diagnostic (Exp-6)** — Part A shows its tall 'gain' is *not* a pure global upward shift (a single predicted-mean scale does not reproduce it) yet the predictor does not track held-out in-domain per-scene scale (Spearman 0.036): a MIXED_INCONCLUSIVE — the per-scene variation is a real but crude tall-tail lever, not image-recoverable calibration.
