# Phase 62 — Training Readiness Gate

## Status

NOT READY FOR FULL TRAINING.

## Preconditions not yet met

- no valid Indian benchmark yet selected
- no real paired optical + elevation benchmark downloaded
- no alignment validation performed
- no geographic split executed on real data
- no real baseline result computed on India

## Required next milestone

A single pilot Indian benchmark must be acquired and aligned. After that, the current model must be run unchanged on the benchmark to establish a real baseline.

## Training design to prepare later

Potential future experimental arms:

1. India-only fine-tuning
2. global + India mixed training
3. terrain-balanced or slope-balanced training

However, these are not to be launched until the benchmark is valid and baseline inference is completed.

## Final summary

The project is still at the acquisition + validation gate. Training readiness remains pending.
